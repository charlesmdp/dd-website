Reviewed 22 September 2026. The accompanying pendora-manifest.json records source URLs, dates, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes. ## Claims safe to publish

- **Pendora is currently listed as free.** Its Shopify listing names Henqq ecommerce LLC, lists English, and advertises email delivery, license keys, custom download pages, watermarks and unlimited downloads. No numeric single-file upload limit, total storage allowance or bandwidth cap was found in its listing or discoverable public developer documentation. Use **“not specified in the public sources reviewed”** for those three figures. Unlimited downloads is not proof of unlimited storage or bandwidth.
  Source: https://apps.shopify.com/pendora-digital-download

- **BIG publishes graduated storage allowances.** Its official Shopify listing shows Free: 250 MB and 50 orders; Starter: $9.99/month, 15 GB storage, 50 GB bandwidth; Pro: $19.99/month, 50 GB storage, 500 GB bandwidth; Growth: $49.99/month, 1 TB storage and 1 TB bandwidth. Paid tiers list unlimited digital orders. Prices are USD. Developer: Penida. Languages include French.
  Source: https://apps.shopify.com/digital-download-products

- **BIG says it supports files of any size, within plan storage, and provides externally hosted download-by-URL.** Treat this as a publisher claim, not independently stress-tested performance.
  Source: https://www.bigdigitaldownload.com/faq

- **Pumper currently has both free and paid options.** Its listing labels the free plan “Free upto $300”. Starter is $9.99/month (up to $1,000 extra revenue generated by Pumper); Growth $29.99/month (up to $5,000); Unlimited $49.99/month. Annual alternatives are also shown. This proves current pricing, not its launch pricing or Pendora’s future.
  Source: https://apps.shopify.com/pumper-quantity-breaks-product-bundles-discounts

## Developer relationship — exact wording matters

The two live Shopify listings do not use the same developer name: Pendora says **Henqq ecommerce LLC**; Pumper says **Profit Pumper Bundle**.

Pumper’s official privacy policy, effective **9 March 2026**, identifies **Proveway Pvt Ltd (India)** and **Henq eCommerce LLC (USA)**. Pendora’s official listing links to a Proveway privacy policy. That linked policy describes the Proveway payment-tracking app, not Pendora specifically. These are verifiable connections, but the spelling differs (“Henq” versus “Henqq”) and the reviewed pages do not resolve the legal entity identity or ownership conclusively. Prefer citing the exact disclosures over an unqualified “same developer”.

## Limits and conflicting claims to avoid

- A Shopify Community merchant post from January 2025 mentions 500 MB; a more recent merchant review mentions 2 GB; a competing vendor also alleges 2 GB and a 30 MB PDF-stamping limit. None is a current Pendora developer specification. Do not publish those as verified upload or storage ceilings.
- Do not claim Pendora lacks branded/custom download pages: its official gallery explicitly advertises one. Its listing also advertises watermarks, so “no PDF protection” is too broad.
- BIG’s FAQ has inconsistent plan statements: it describes PDF stamping as both a reason to move to paid plans and available from Free. The Shopify price table explicitly lists it on Starter. Confirm that detail with product knowledge before assigning it to a plan in comparison copy.
- Do not turn individual negative reviews into categorical claims about reliability, misconduct or review manipulation. No installed-app test was performed.
