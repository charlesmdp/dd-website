Introduction

PENIDA SAS (“We,” “Us,” “Our”) is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share personal data when you install or use our app BIG Digital Download, a digital product distribution service available through the Shopify App Store at this link. Our registered office is located at 14 RUE CHARLES-V, 75004 PARIS, France, SIRET number 88033708400037. This policy applies specifically to BIG Digital Download and explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under various privacy regulations, including GDPR and CCPA

Personal Identification Data

  1. Name

  2. Email Address

  3. Billing Information: Payment details used to process transactions.

Shopify Store Information

  1. Store Name

  2. Domain

  3. Order Data: Details about transactions made through your Shopify store.

  4. Product Data: Information about the digital products you upload and sell.

Technical Data

  1. IP Addresses

  2. Device Information: Type of device, operating system, and browser.

  3. Usage Statistics: Information about how our app is used, including bandwidth consumption.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  1. Provide and Maintain the Service: This includes using your Shopify data to offer digital downloads to your customers and tracking your storage and bandwidth usage.

  2. Improve Our Services: Analyzing user behavior to optimize app features and performance.

  3. Customer Support: Using your contact information to respond to support requests and offer technical assistance.

  4. Notify You of Changes: Updates or changes to our service will be communicated to you via email or through the app.

  5. Compliance with Legal Obligations: We may use your information to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

We also use your data to monitor bandwidth consumption, especially for those on the ULTRA plan, where bandwidth limits apply. For more details on bandwidth policies, refer to the Fair Use Policy on Bandwidth.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as follows:

  1. Free Users: Data is retained for 30 days after trial expiration or uninstallation.

  2. Paying Customers: Data is retained for 30 days post-contract termination, unless you request earlier deletion.

  3. We may retain some data longer if required for legal, accounting, or billing purposes.

Upon uninstallation, all store-related data, including customer data and product files, is automatically deleted. We do not store your Shopify login credentials.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to host and maintain our app:

  1. DigitalOcean: The app and associated data are hosted on DigitalOcean servers.

  2. Shopify: Your store data is shared with us through Shopify’s API. We comply with Shopify’s data-sharing policies.

We do not sell your data to third parties. Any data sharing that occurs is strictly for the functionality of the service.

Security

We take commercially reasonable measures to secure your personal information. While we use industry-standard practices to protect your data, no method of internet transmission or storage is 100% secure. By using our service, you acknowledge that we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously monitor and update our security practices to mitigate risks.

Fair Use Policy on Bandwidth

Our BIG Digital Download app offers different plans, each with a specified storage limit and bandwidth allocation. The ULTRA plan, for instance, provides 1,000GB of bandwidth per month. You can monitor your bandwidth consumption in real time through your App Dashboard, accessible via this link, replacing "NAMEOFTHESTORE" with your store's actual name.


Overage Fees: If you exceed the bandwidth limit on any plan, a fee of $0.023 per additional GB will be charged.


Exceeding the bandwidth allocation could reclassify your account from a standard digital product distributor to a high-traffic data transfer site, which goes beyond the scope of our intended service. We apologize for this inconvenience, but our infrastructure and legal framework are not designed to handle such high levels of bandwidth usage. In such cases, we may temporarily limit your ability to upload or distribute additional products until bandwidth usage returns to acceptable levels.

Your Rights: GDPR and CCPA Compliance

For EU Users (GDPR)

  1. Right to Access: You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you.

  2. Right to Rectification: You may correct or update inaccurate information.

  3. Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal data.

  4. Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a portable format.

  5. Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your personal data.

For California Users (CCPA):

  1. Right to Access: You have the right to know what personal information we collect.

  2. Right to Delete: You may request deletion of your personal data.

  3. Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to opt-out of the sale of your personal data (though we do not sell data).

f you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@penidapify.com or penidastudio@gmail.com 

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. If changes are made, we will notify you via email or through the app. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, please contact us at:


  1. Email: hello@penidapify.com or penidastudio@gmail.com 

  2. Support Page: Available through the app interface.


This Privacy Policy applies to all users of BIG Digital Download and our other Shopify apps. By using our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy and any future updates.
